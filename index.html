About

I'm a PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science at Virginia Tech, expected to graduate in December 2026, and I completed my MSc in the same department in May 2025 with a 4.00 GPA. My research analyzes large-scale EHR, clinical, and genomic datasets to support precision medicine — building reproducible pipelines for healthcare data integration and developing predictive, explainable models for prognosis and risk stratification, with a current focus on metabolic and liver disease.

Alongside my PhD, I've completed two internship terms as a Bioinformatics intern in the Division of Computational Biology at Mayo Clinic, and taught undergraduate courses in machine learning, data mining, and software engineering as a Lecturer at Daffodil International University. Before returning to academia, I worked as a System Engineer and Senior Software Engineer building web applications and IoT-based data systems.
