About

Tahmina Sultana Priya is a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at Virginia Tech. Her research is driven by a simple question: how can we make machine learning reliable and understandable enough to actually help clinicians make better decisions? Her work focuses on machine learning for healthcare, explainable AI, and precision medicine. She is particularly interested in developing models that can help with prognosis, identify meaningful patient subgroups, and better understand clinical outcomes in complex diseases. What she enjoys most about research is working with real-world clinical data and finding patterns that can lead to insights that are useful in practice. During her research journey, she has worked as a bioinformatics research intern and collaborated with the Mayo Clinic, gaining experience working with electronic health records and multi-omics data. These experiences have strengthened her interest in using computational methods to address challenging problems in healthcare and precision medicine.

She earned her M.S. in Computer Science from Virginia Tech and her B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology in Bangladesh.She is currently preparing for her Ph.D. defense and looking for postdoctoral or research opportunities where she can continue exploring meaningful problems at the intersection of machine learning and healthcare. In the long term, she hopes to build a career in academia, where she can pursue independent research, mentor students, and contribute to research that has a meaningful impact on people’s lives.

Tahmina is a hardworking and independent researcher who takes her work seriously and has a strong sense of responsibility toward what she does. She values honesty, consistency, and strong work ethics in her research and professional life. At the same time, she enjoys meeting new people, spending time with friends, traveling, and exploring new places.
